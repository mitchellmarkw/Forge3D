# Test sheet: 0.94.0, Slice 85: Seams you paint, for sewing patterns

Time needed: about 15 minutes. (Includes slices 82–84 until those pull requests are merged.)

## Get the build

Open the pull request **Slice 85: Seams you paint, for sewing patterns (0.94.0)**, open its Windows build check,
download the `Forge3D-windows-x64-…` artifact, unzip and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Use a copy of the figure with a shirt painted on it from the slice 79 test.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Paint: in a second colour, paint a thin line (a small brush) from the armpit down the side, each side; and one round each sleeve where it meets the body | Lines over the shirt | |
| 2 | Select it; Sewing Pattern; Painted colour: the shirt's | Under Seams, "Also cut along lines painted" and "Seam lines' colour" (label on top of its box) | |
| 3 | Seams: One piece; tick Also cut along lines painted; Seam lines' colour: the lines' | The body in two (front and back) and each sleeve a piece of its own, unrolled | |
| 4 | Look at the stretch figure | Lower than without the lines (the armpit's cut now) | |
| 5 | Seams: Front and back | Cut at the side seams and along your lines too | |
| 6 | Untick | Back to the plain pattern | |
| 7 | Choose the shirt's own colour as the lines' colour | "The seam lines need a colour of their own." | |
| 8 | Paint a line on the figure away from the shirt (in the lines' colour); tick again | No change from that one | |
| 9 | Save as PDF; print at 100%; cut out and tape round the figure | The pieces wrap it, meeting along your lines | |
