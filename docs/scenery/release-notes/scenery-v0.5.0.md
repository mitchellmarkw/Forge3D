The first release of **Forge3D Scenery**, the companion app to Forge3D for scenery: buildings, rocks, terrain and trees, designed from real-world sizes and made at your model's scale. It shares Forge3D's engine, 3D view and .f3d files, so a model opens in either app. A beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-Scenery-0.5.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). It installs beside Forge3D, not over it.
- **Forge3D-Scenery-0.5.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D Scenery.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## What it does

- **Model scale** (Scenery tab): 1:6 to 1:220, 1:60 among them, or your own (1:76, 1:87.1). Everything is made to it.
- **New Building**: walls of brick (real brick sizes, running bond, mortar or tight joints), stone blocks, logs or wood planks; a flat, gable or hip roof, its own shape or joined; doors and windows added by clicking on a wall and dragged to move.
- **New Rock**: a boulder, crag, slab or pile of rocks; roughness, bedding layers, sunk into the ground with a flat base; New Shape for another like it.
- **New Terrain**: a rectangle or round/oval base, plain or bevelled edge; rolling hills, level at the edges so pieces line up; craters, crags and paths placed by a click (dragged to move) or scattered at random.
- **New Tree**: broadleaf, conifer, dead or palm; height, crown, trunk, branches, lean; lumpy foliage clumps made to print, or bare; flared roots cut flat. A new tree is sized to fit under your printer.
- **Change** (or double-click in Objects) reopens any of them with its settings, to change it where it is. Undo takes it back.
- **Smallest detail**: tell it your printer's finest detail; mortar, branches and surfaces are kept printable.
- Open, save and export (3MF, STL) as in Forge3D; light and dark themes; Check for Updates (Scenery's own releases).
