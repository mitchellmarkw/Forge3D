# Forge3D Scenery roadmap

Scenery for models and dioramas, built to scale, in the same .f3d files as Forge3D.

| Slice | What | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 (Forge3D 110) | Groundwork: the app, its window, opening/saving/exporting .f3d, model scale, its own installer and releases | Done |
| S2 (Forge3D 111) | Building designer: brick, stone blocks, logs or planks from real sizes at the model scale, mortar or none; flat, gable or hip roof; doors and windows by a click; changeable | Done (0.2.0) |
| S3 (Forge3D 112) | Rocks: boulder, crag, slab or pile from real sizes; roughness, layers, sunk with a flat base; a new shape at a click; changeable | Done (0.3.0) |
| S4 (Forge3D 113) | Terrain: rectangle or round base, plain or bevelled; rolling hills; craters, crags and paths by a click or scattered; changeable | Done (0.4.0) |
| S5 (Forge3D 114) | Trees: broadleaf, conifer, dead or palm; height, crown, trunk, branches, lean; foliage clumps or bare; flared roots; changeable | Done (0.5.0) |
| S6 (Forge3D 115) | Building shapes: L, T, U and courtyard footprints, joined walls, a roof over each wing; sized by fields or by dragging the corners | Done |
| S7 (Forge3D 116) | Wall materials: rough-hewn stone (rock-faced, random courses) and fieldstone (irregular, "giraffe" stonework) | Done |
| S8 (Forge3D 117) | Battlements along wall tops; towers (round or square; flat, battlements or a roof) placed at corners or on walls, dragged; each tower in its own material (0.8.1) | **Delivered for testing** |

Small details are checked against the printer: a mortar line too fine to print becomes an engraved line, or is
warned about.
