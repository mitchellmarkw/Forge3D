# Forge3D Scenery roadmap

Scenery for models and dioramas, built to scale, in the same .f3d files as Forge3D.

| Slice | What | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 (Forge3D 110) | Groundwork: the app, its window, opening/saving/exporting .f3d, model scale, its own installer and releases | **Delivered for testing** |
| S2 | Building designer: walls of brick, stone or logs from real sizes at the model scale, mortar or none; doors and windows; editable steps | Planned |
| S3 | Rocks and boulders | Planned |
| S4 | Terrain: ground, craters, crags | Planned |
| S5 | Tree designer: deciduous or evergreen, limb density, size, leaves or bare | Planned |

Small details are checked against the printer: a mortar line too fine to print becomes an engraved line, or is
warned about.
