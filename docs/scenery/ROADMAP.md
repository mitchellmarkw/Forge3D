# Forge3D Scenery roadmap

Scenery for models and dioramas, built to scale, in the same .f3d files as Forge3D.

| Slice | What | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 (Forge3D 110) | Groundwork: the app, its window, opening/saving/exporting .f3d, model scale, its own installer and releases | Done |
| S2 (Forge3D 111) | Building designer: brick, stone blocks, logs or planks from real sizes at the model scale, mortar or none; flat, gable or hip roof; doors and windows by a click; changeable | Done (0.2.0) |
| S3 (Forge3D 112) | Rocks: boulder, crag, slab or pile from real sizes; roughness, layers, sunk with a flat base; a new shape at a click; changeable | **Delivered for testing** |
| S4 | Terrain: ground, craters, crags | Planned |
| S5 | Tree designer: deciduous or evergreen, limb density, size, leaves or bare | Planned |

Small details are checked against the printer: a mortar line too fine to print becomes an engraved line, or is
warned about.
