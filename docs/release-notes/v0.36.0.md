Forge3D 0.36 Beta adds a **Pattern brush** for sculpting, a **Measure** tool, **screw threads**, and **loft** and **sweep** shapes in the sketcher. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.36.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.32 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.36.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.32

- **Pattern brush** (Sculpt): press a repeating pattern into the surface (Scales, Hexagons, Woven cloth, Skin, Bricks, or your own picture). Going over the same place again doesn't press any deeper; Ctrl engraves.
- **Measure** (Build, Design and Print tabs): click faces, edges, corners or points to see areas, diameters and lengths, and between two of them the distance, angle, whether they're parallel, and centre to centre of holes and pegs. It only looks; nothing in the model changes.
- **Thread** (Design tab): cut a metric screw thread (M2 to M30, or any size) into a peg or a hole. A **Fit** setting loosens it so printed parts screw together, and there's a left-hand option. It's a step you can change later, and it follows the peg or hole when that changes.
- **Loft** (sketcher, **Make:**): blend one outline into others stacked above it, such as a square base to a round top, a vase or a funnel. Smooth or straight sides.
- **Sweep** (sketcher, **Make:**): move an outline along a path you draw (pipes, handles, bent rods) or round a spiral (springs and coils, with pitch, turns and left-hand).

Files saved with 0.36 need 0.36 or later to open. Files from earlier versions open as before.
