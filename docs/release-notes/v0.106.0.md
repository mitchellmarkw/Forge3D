Forge3D 0.106 Beta adds tools from how real action figures are made: **Make Part** paints armor, gloves and belts straight onto a figure, **toy joint recipes** cut a figure the way poseable toys are built, **Sculpt strength** tones a whole sculpt step up or down, and an **eye in the Objects list** shows and hides shapes. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.106.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.102 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.106.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.102

**Make Part (Figure Design tab)**

- Paint where armor, a glove, a boot or a belt goes, and a new separate part is made there, following the body's surface and the edge of the paint.
- **Sits on top** (with a fit gap, like real armor) or **Set into the body**: the body is cut back under the part so it sits flush, with the outline unchanged. Choose its thickness and fit.

**Toy joint recipes in Cut into Parts**

- **Butterfly shoulder**, **drop-down hip**, **ankle rocker** and **ab crunch**: each puts a small block in at the joint, with a joint on each side of it, the way poseable toys are built.
- **Super Poseable**: one click for a toy's full set: neck ball, butterfly shoulders, ab crunch, drop-down hips, double elbows and knees, and ankle rockers.

**Sculpt strength**

- Click a sculpt step and a **Sculpt strength** slider (0–150%) tones every stroke in it down or pushes them further together. 100% always gives back exactly what you sculpted, and changing it is quick even on heavy sculpts.

**Show and hide**

- An **eye** by every shape in Objects: click it to hide the shape from the view, again to show it. Right-click for **Show Only This** and **Show All**, or use **Ctrl+H** and **Ctrl+Shift+H**.
- Hiding only changes what you see: hidden shapes are still saved, exported and printed.

## File format

Files from 0.102 open in 0.106. Files saved by 0.106 with a sculpt step not at 100% strength need 0.106 to open.
