Forge3D 0.112 Beta smooths two rough edges from 0.111. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.112.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.111 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.112.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.111

- **Names without a needless number**: a new shape or step is called just "Box", "Posed", "Sword"... while no other shape has that name; the next is "Box 2", then "Box 3". Shapes already in your files keep their names.
- **Cut into Parts with Print in place**: the line under each joint now shows what will be built: the clearance (not the usual fit), and that the ball is held in or the pin is part of the fork. It follows the clearance as you change it.

## File format

Unchanged from 0.111.
