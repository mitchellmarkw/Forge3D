Forge3D 0.73 Beta adds the **figure, toy and scale-model tools**: ready-made joints (sockets, hinges, swivels, clicks, magnet pockets), test prints to dial in your printer's fit, a figure guide to build on, Print Check, hollowing for resin, rivets and panel lines, Split by Mask for painting, kit layouts, and a parts list. It also opens PLY and glTF files, adds colours, and makes sculpting on big meshes faster. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.73.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.47 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.73.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

New to the figure tools? **Help → Figure, Toy and Kit Tools** says what each one does and where to find it.

## New since 0.47

**Modelling and viewing**

- **Colours** for each shape, kept in the file and in 3MF exports.
- **Smooth**, **Extrude Down**, and **Solid / See-Through / Wireframe** views.
- **Open PLY and glTF / GLB** files.
- **Split to Fit** shows its cuts before it makes them, and can cut at an angle.
- **Lay out pieces on plates**: spreads many parts over as few build plates as will hold them.
- **Faster sculpting on big meshes.**
- **Custom shape library**: save your own shapes and drop them in again.
- **Join Parts**: click a place on one part and where it goes on another, and it moves there (faces flat together, a peg into its hole).
- **Reference Images** behind the model, sized from two points.
- **Scale Calculator** (Tools menu): a real size at 1 : X, in the unit you choose.

**Joints and fit**

- **Quick Socket**: a ball-and-socket joint at the spot you click.
- **Quick Hinge**: single and double hinges, and swivels, with printed, filament or wire pins.
- **Clicks and ridges** so a joint holds a pose, and **magnet pockets**.
- **Pose Test**: turn a figure's joints to see how far they go before parts hit.
- **Tolerance Test Print**: a small print that tells you which fit your printer needs.

**Figures and detail**

- **Figure Guide**: a posable mannequin to the size and proportions you choose, to sculpt or build over.
- **Stamp**: rivets, bolts, screws, studs, vents, scales, stitches or your own shapes, raised or sunk, one at a time or in rows.
- **Panel Lines**: V, square or round grooves along lines you click. Hold **Shift** for a level line, **Ctrl** for an upright one.

**Getting it printed**

- **Print Check**: shows walls too thin to print and overhangs that need supports.
- **Hollow** for resin: walls of a set thickness, drain holes, and plugs.
- **Simplify** to a number of triangles, showing the STL size.
- **Split by Mask**: paint where to cut, for painting parts separately, with alignment keys so the pieces glue back only one way.
- **Rescale Design**: makes a whole design bigger or smaller while keeping pegs, pins and wall thicknesses the size that prints.
- **Kit Layout**: small parts in rows on a frame like a model kit's sprue, gated, numbered and named.
- **Parts List**: each part's size, volume, weight and cost, copied to a spreadsheet or saved as CSV or PDF.

## File format

Files from 0.47 open in 0.73. Files saved by 0.73 that use the new tools need 0.73 to open.
