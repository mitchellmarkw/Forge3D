Forge3D 0.99 Beta is the **figure-maker's release**: bend and pose a sculpted figure, cut it into parts with the joints put in, swappable hands, paint for color printing, design variables shared across a whole figure line, sewing patterns and instruction sheets for kits, molds for casting, and a reorganized ribbon with new **Figure Design** and **Tools** tabs. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.99.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.73 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.99.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

**Things have moved.** The ribbon now has a **Figure Design** tab (Cut into Parts, Make Hand, Pose Figure, Figure Guide) and a **Tools** tab (Variables, Measure, Print Check, Will It Stand?, Pose Test, Rotate for Best Print, Manage Library). Paint, Color, Stamp and Panel Lines are on **Design → Look**. Only Undo and Redo are on every tab. **Help → Figure, Toy and Kit Tools** says where everything is.

## New since 0.73

**Figures**

- **Pose Figure**: bend a figure sculpted in one piece (or an imported one) at its joints.
- **Cut into Parts**: cuts a one-piece figure at its joints and puts the joints in (ball joints or hinges), with each cut adjustable by hand.
- **Make Hand**: a hand at your figure's scale, ready to print or sculpt further.
- **Hand Ports**: pegs and grip holes for swappable hands and accessories, sized from your joint standard.
- **Joint Standards** (Tools menu): a figure line's joint sizes kept by name, so every part in the line fits.
- **Linked Copy on the Other Side** (Design → Mirror): a mirrored left/right part that keeps up with changes to the original.

**Joints and fit**

- **Pose Test** now has **Make Room**, which cuts away just what's in the way so a joint turns further, and **Make a Display Base** for the figure as posed.
- **Will It Stand?**: shows the centre of weight and whether a pose will stand, with a peg hole in each foot for a stand.
- **Design variables** (Tools → Variables): named sizes used across many shapes and joints. Change one and everything that uses it follows.
- **Quick Hinge** pieces (tabs, links, pegs, pins) follow their hinge when it changes.
- **A joint at a split**: Split and Split by Mask can put in a ball joint, hinge, double hinge, swivel or magnets where the pieces meet.

**Detail and color**

- **Paint**: brush colors onto a shape, exported in 3MF for color printing.
- **Split by Mask** can mask from a painted color, and cut **curved**, **flat**, or with a **ball and socket** that lines the pieces up.
- **Texture stamps**: chainmail, denim, knitting and more for the sculpting Pattern brush, pressed once or repeated.

**Getting it made**

- **Rotate for Best Print**: turns parts the best way up for the fastest print, the best quality, or the strongest part, and shows how it compares before you rotate.
- **Make Mold**: two-, three- or four-part mold boxes for casting resin copies, showing undercuts as you go.
- **Instruction Sheet**: a PDF to go with a kit, its parts numbered as in the Parts List.
- **Sewing Pattern**: cloth clothes for a figure from paint on it, with seams where you paint them.
- **Picture**: a picture of the model, or of a pose, for posts or a shop listing.
- **Manage Library** (Tools): rename or delete your saved custom shapes.

## File format

Files from 0.73 open in 0.99. Files saved by 0.99 that use the new tools need 0.99 to open.
