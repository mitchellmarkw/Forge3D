Forge3D 0.29 Beta brings **sculpting** (the start of Phase 2) and a **view cube**. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.29.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.25 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.29.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.25

- **Sculpt** (Sculpt tab): push, pull and smooth any shape like clay, with seven brushes: Draw, Smooth, Inflate, Flatten, Pinch, Clay and Grab. Ctrl does the opposite, Shift smooths, [ and ] change the size.
- **Mirror** X / Y / Z: sculpt one side and the other follows.
- **Detail where you sculpt**: triangles are added under the brush as you go, so fine strokes stay crisp without making the whole shape heavy. Fast even on meshes of a million triangles.
- **View cube** in the corner of the 3D view: shows which side faces you; click a side, edge or corner to look from there; buttons to turn 90° and go back to the starting view. View → View Cube turns it off.

Sculpting turns the shape into a mesh, so its earlier steps can't be changed afterwards (Undo puts it back). Keeping sculpting in the history is the next step.
