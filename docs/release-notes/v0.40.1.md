Forge3D 0.40 Beta adds **sketch tools** (trim, round corner, offset, mirror), **text in sketches**, **taper** on pads and pockets, and a **section view** for looking inside models. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.40.1-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.36 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.40.1-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.36

- **Sketch tools** on the sketcher's toolbar:
  - **Trim** (X): click a piece of a line or curve to take it away, back to where other lines cross it.
  - **Round corner** (Shift+R): turn picked corners into arcs with a radius you can change later.
  - **Offset** (Shift+O): copy a whole outline a set distance out or in. **Gap** dimensions hold the distance, so the copy follows the original.
  - **Mirror** (M): copy things to the other side of a line; the copy stays a mirror image.
- **Text in sketches** (W): type words in any installed font. Pad them for standing letters, pocket them on a face to engrave, or draw an outline round them to cut them out like a stencil. Double-click the words to change them.
- **Taper** on pads and pockets: an angle that leans the sides in (or out) as they go away from the sketch, the draft that moulded parts have. Pockets get narrower as they go deeper.
- **Section View** (View menu): cut the view open across X, Y or Z to look inside, for everything on the plate or only the selected shape. Only the view is cut; the model, exports and prints stay whole.

Files saved with 0.40 need 0.40 or later to open. Files from earlier versions open as before.
