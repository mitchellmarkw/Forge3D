Forge3D 0.116 Beta adds quick figure styles and a brick figure maker to the Figure Design tab. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.116.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.112 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.116.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.112

- **Figure Style** (Figure Design → Position): turn a one-piece figure into a **Bobblehead**, a **Vinyl collectible** or a **Caricature** in one go, with a Strength slider. It opens Pose Figure on a new **4. Style** stage, so you can fine-tune the proportions and pose it in the same step.
  - **Bobblehead** can cut the head off to sit on a spring: a socket in the head and a post on the neck, sized for your spring. Choose where the head comes off with a flat cut at the neck (an orange disc you move up or down and tip), or paint a mask over the head.
- **Brick Figure** (Figure Design → Figure): the figure's own head on a new body in the build of the brick-compatible figures: tapered torso, hips with an axle for the legs, arms bent forward with C-shaped hands that take Accessories' handles. Place the head's cut with the orange disc, nudge the head forward or back, and build it as parts that go together or in one piece.
- **Proportions** gain **Limb thickness** and **Body width**.
- **The neck is found from the figure's shape**: the head's cut goes under the chin, where the neck is narrowest, even on figures with big heads.
- A bigger head no longer flares the neck into a lip under it.
- Pose Figure's stage buttons sit two to a row, so their names fit.

## File format

54: posed steps save limb thickness and body width. Files from 0.112 open as before; files saved with 0.116 need 0.116 or later.
