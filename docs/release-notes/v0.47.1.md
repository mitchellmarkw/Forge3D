Forge3D 0.47 Beta adds **splitting for printing**: alignment pegs that key a cut so the pieces go together only one way, and **Split to Fit**, which cuts a model too big for your printer into pieces that fit. It also adds a **dark theme** and **sketching on a section view's cut**. A code audit makes damaged files and extreme sizes safe. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.47.1-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.44 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.47.1-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.44

- **Alignment pegs on Split**: when you keep both halves, tapered pegs stand out of one piece into matching holes in the other. **Loose pins** is the other choice: holes in both pieces, with pins to print separately. You can change the diameter, length, taper and clearance, and your settings are remembered. If a cut is too narrow for pegs, Forge3D says so and offers to undo the split.
- **Split to Fit** (Print/Save tab, Edit menu, or **Smart Split** in Split): cuts a shape too big for your build plate into as few pieces as will fit. Each cut goes where it cuts through least, and every cut is keyed. One Undo puts it back.
- **Clearer fit warning**: orange-red means the shape is too big for the plate (Split to Fit will help); amber means it is off the plate but would fit if moved.
- **Dark theme** (View → Theme: Follow Windows, Light or Dark): switches straight away, with no restart.
- **Sketch on the section's cut**: with Section View on, click the cut face, then Sketch. Its outline is there to snap to. A Pocket from there cuts into the half you can see, so you can make hidden chambers and channels.

## Fixes

- Damaged project files and damaged STL, OBJ or 3MF imports now give a plain message instead of crashing Forge3D or leaving it busy.
- Sizes that can't work are refused straight away instead of leaving Forge3D busy:
  - an edge rounding bigger than the shape;
  - a thread pitch far too fine for its length;
  - more than 120 letters in one piece of text.
- One shape that can't be built no longer hides the others.
- An unexpected error shows a message and Forge3D keeps running, so you can save your work.
- Deleting everything now clears the backup copy, so an older version isn't offered back after a crash.

No change to the file format: files from 0.44 open in 0.47.
