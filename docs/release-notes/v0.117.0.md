Forge3D 0.117 Beta makes Brick Figure easier to get right: see the brick figure as you set it up, and choose the head with a mask. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.117.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.116 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.117.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.116

- **See the brick figure while you set it up**: Brick Figure shows the brick figure beside your figure, where it will be made, following every change (the cut, head size and position, height, build). Untick **Show the brick figure beside the figure** to hide it.
- **Choose the head with a mask**: under **Choose the head by**, pick **A mask painted over the head**, click **Paint the Head...**, paint the head (or lasso round it from the front), then **Use This Mask**. The cut goes flat through the paint's edge.
