Forge3D 0.102 Beta makes splitting quicker and smarter: **Split by Mask is much faster** on detailed figures, **joints at a split now follow their cut**, and **Rotate for Best Print knows resin printers**. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.102.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.99 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.102.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.99

**Faster Split by Mask**

- The surface is now only made finer where the paint actually starts or stops, not all over the painted area where strokes overlap, and the middle of each cut's cap is no finer than it needs to be.
- On a test figure painted with many small strokes, a split went from 7.3 seconds and 2.7 million triangles to 0.4 seconds and 77,000, with the same pieces. Flat cuts, ball and socket, and joints at the cut all gain from it, and the pieces are much lighter to save and export.

**Joints that follow their cut**

- A joint put in by Split or Split by Mask (ball joint, hinge, double hinge, swivel or magnets) now moves with its cut. **Move the cut** or **change the mask**, and the joint goes in again at the middle of the new cut, sized to fit if it was set to "Fit the cut". One undo puts it all back.
- Moving either piece's cut moves the other's too, and **Move the cut...** now works from inside a shape's history when the cut has a joint.
- Changing the mask of a jointed split now updates both pieces.

**Rotate for Best Print, for resin too**

- A **Printer** choice: **Filament (FDM)** or **Resin**. On resin, Fastest Print is simply the lowest height, and Best Quality keeps supports off the surface and the layers small (a big layer pulls hard on the film), so parts usually come out tipped.
- **Turn it to take the least room on the plate**: after finding the best way up, it turns the part on the plate to take as little room as it can, its long side left to right.
- The comparison table shows each part's biggest layer.

## File format

Files from 0.99 open in 0.102. Files saved by 0.102 with a joint at a split need 0.102 to open.
