Forge3D 0.111 Beta finishes the action-figure tool set: **Lasso Cut** and lasso masks, **Accessories** made at your figure's scale, **Proportions** (heroic, chibi or your own), a **Reshape** box to bend any shape, and **print-in-place joints** for printing a figure already put together. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.111.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.106 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.111.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.106

**Lasso Cut and lasso masks**

- **Lasso Cut** (Figure Design tab): drag a loop round part of a shape and cut straight through the way you're looking: cut away inside the loop, keep only inside it, or both pieces. The loop is kept as a sketch, so double-clicking the step changes it later.
- **Lasso** in Split by Mask and Make Part paints everything inside a loop, front and back; **Lasso mask** does the same when sculpting. Ctrl rubs out.

**Accessories (Figure Design tab)**

- A blade, shield, staff or spear, belt with buckle, or holster at your figure's scale, from life-size measurements. Grips, staffs and shield bars match the hands' grip holes.
- **Your accessories**: save any shape as one of yours and add it again any time, like custom shapes.

**Proportions (Figure Design tab, or Pose Figure → 3. Proportions)**

- Head, shoulders, torso, arms, hands, legs and feet from 30% to 300%, or **Heroic** and **Chibi** in one click. The joints stay joined, and the figure can be posed in the same step.

**Reshape (Sculpt tab)**

- A box of handles round any shape: drag a handle and the shape bends smoothly with it. 2, 3 or 4 handles a side, left and right mirrored if you like. Double-click the step to move them again.

**Print in place (Cut into Parts)**

- Tick **Print in place (filament)** and every joint is built captive with a clearance all round, so the figure prints already put together and moves once it's off the plate. Start at 0.35 mm; go bigger if joints print stuck, smaller if loose.

**Fixed**

- On some figures the skeleton's balls in Cut into Parts and Pose Figure wouldn't move with "Move both sides together" off.

## File format

Files from 0.106 open in 0.111. Files saved by 0.111 that use lasso masks, proportions or Reshape need 0.111 to open.
