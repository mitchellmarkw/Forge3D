Forge3D 0.44 Beta reorganises the ribbon (with a new **View** tab), adds **recent files**, a **build plate you can size to your printer** with a warning when shapes don't fit, **backup copies** that are offered back after a crash, and measurements shown in the view. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.44.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.40 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.44.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.40

- **The ribbon, reorganised**: Build, Design, Sculpt, View and Print/Save. Combine and Arrange are now on Design, with Lay on Face and Sit on Plate; Measure and Undo are on every tab.
- **View tab**: **Orbit**, **Pan** and **Zoom** put the view on the left mouse button until you click them again (for touchpads and mice without a middle button), plus Fit, Reset View, Section View and Plate Size.
- **File → Open Recent**: the last six models opened or saved.
- **Build Plate Size**: set the grid to your printer's bed and height, from common sizes or typed in. Shapes that reach past the plate or above the height turn orange-red, and the status bar counts them.
- **Backup copies**: unsaved work is backed up every few minutes (2 to 60, or off, in Edit → Backup Copies). If Forge3D doesn't close properly, the next start offers to recover it. Your own files are never touched until you save.
- **Section View**: clicks now land on what you can see, not on the hidden part.
- **Measure**: distances are written on their lines in the view, and **Keep on Screen** leaves several showing at once.

No change to the file format: files from 0.40 open in 0.44 and the other way round.
