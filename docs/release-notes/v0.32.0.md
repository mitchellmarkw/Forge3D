Forge3D 0.32 Beta adds three sculpting brushes, a **mask**, **Remesh** and **Simplify**. Still a beta: keep copies of models you care about.

## Download

- **Forge3D-0.32.0-Setup.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installing over 0.30 updates it in place.
- **Forge3D-0.32.0-portable.zip**: unzip anywhere and run `Forge3D.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).

## New since 0.30

- **Mask** brush: paint over parts you want to protect (they turn dark), and the other brushes leave them alone. Ctrl unpaints; **Clear mask** and **Invert mask** work on the whole shape.
- **Crease** brush: a sharp, narrow groove for folds and seams (Ctrl: a sharp ridge).
- **Snake hook** brush: pull horns, tails and tentacles out after the mouse; works with Mirror.
- **Remesh** (in the Sculpt panel): rebuilds the whole shape from even triangles of the size you set, tidying areas stretched by Snake hook or Grab.
- **Simplify** (Sculpt tab): fewer triangles for big shapes (imported scans and models, or sculpted shapes) while keeping their look. It's a step: set **Keep: % of the triangles** in Properties, or use **Remove the simplifying** to get the full shape back.
- Sculpted shapes follow the shape under them better: strokes stay on the surface when you make it bigger or smaller.

Files saved with 0.32 need 0.32 or later to open. Files from earlier versions open as before.
